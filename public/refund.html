Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Thank you for choosing Pixel Isolate. We want every creator, e-commerce merchant, and print-on-demand designer to have a transparent and reliable experience with our background isolation and 8K upscaling tools. Please review our cancellation and refund guidelines below.

1. Subscription Cancellations

You may cancel your Pro Tier subscription at any time directly from your Billing & Subscription dashboard or via the Paddle customer billing portal link in your receipt email. When you cancel, automatic renewal is stopped immediately, and you retain full Pro access—including unlimited 4K/8K upscaling, bulk batch processing, and custom solid backdrops—until the end of your current paid billing cycle.

2. 14-Day Refund Eligibility

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on new Pro Tier subscriptions and unused credit bundles when you experience technical issues that prevent normal use of the service, accidental duplicate charges, or billing errors. Refund requests are evaluated promptly on a case-by-case basis within 14 calendar days of the original transaction date.

  • Subscriptions: Full refunds are available within 14 days if the platform fails to function as described on a supported browser and our support team cannot resolve the issue.
  • Credit Bundles: One-time 100 Credit Bundles never expire and are eligible for a full refund within 14 days provided the credits remain substantially unused.

3. How to Request a Refund

All payments are processed by Paddle as our Merchant of Record. To request a refund or resolve a billing question, email contact@pixelisolate.online with your account email address, Paddle order ID, and a brief description of the issue. Approved refunds are issued back to your original payment method and typically appear within 3–5 business days depending on your card issuer.
