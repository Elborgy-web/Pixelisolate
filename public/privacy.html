Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

At Pixel Isolate (operated by Mohamed Elborgy as a sole proprietor brand), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data and creative assets you process through our platform.

1. On-Device Background Removal Architecture

Pixel Isolate is engineered around a privacy-first client-side execution model. When you use our AI Magic segmentation engine or HSV chroma keyer in the workspace, neural inference and morphological edge processing run directly inside your local web browser memory using WebAssembly and WebGPU. Your input images are not harvested or used to train third-party AI models.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account or subscribe to a paid plan, we collect only the information required to operate the service:

  • Account Credentials: Your email address and basic profile display details used for authentication, credit tracking, and optional blog notifications.
  • Saved History Gallery Assets: For authenticated users who save exports to their cloud history gallery, original and isolated image assets are encrypted client-side with AES-256-GCM before storage and retained in private storage buckets solely so you can re-download your files across sessions.
  • Billing Metadata: Subscription status, credit balances, and transaction identifiers provided by our payment processor. We never store raw credit card numbers on our servers.

3. Data Security & Retention

We implement robust, industry-standard security measures—including HTTPS transport encryption, Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS), Row-Level Security (RLS) database policies, and client-side Web Crypto encryption—to safeguard your personal details and saved assets against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. You may delete any saved image from your History Gallery at any time, which permanently removes both the database record and underlying object storage file.

4. Third-Party Processors

We partner with trusted infrastructure providers to deliver core features: Supabase for authentication and database management, Cloudflare for edge delivery and encrypted object storage, and Paddle as our Merchant of Record for secure global payments and tax compliance. These processors manage data strictly in accordance with their respective privacy and security commitments.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Contact

In accordance with GDPR, CCPA, and applicable data protection laws, you have the right to access, export, correct, or request deletion of your personal account data, and to unsubscribe from email notifications at any time. For privacy inquiries or deletion requests, contact us at contact@pixelisolate.online.
